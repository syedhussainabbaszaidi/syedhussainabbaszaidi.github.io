08 / FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONSGOOD TO KNOW BEFORE YOU START

Questions before
we get started.

Is the advertising budget included in the package?

No. The monthly package covers the agreed management and deliverables. Meta or Google advertising spend is paid separately by you, directly to the advertising platform.

Can I start with one service only?

Yes. If you need SEO only, ads management only, content writing or social media support, message us and we can discuss a focused scope.

Do you guarantee leads, sales or Google rankings?

No responsible marketer can guarantee a fixed number of leads, sales or rankings. We focus on sound setup, relevant targeting, useful content, measurement and ongoing improvement.

Can I start with the Starter package and upgrade later?

Yes. You can begin with the Starter package and move to a higher plan when your content, audience or campaign requirements grow.

What do you need from me to get started?

Usually your business details, service or product information, target locations, social page access, brand assets and a clear idea of your goals. Ad account access is needed only if we are managing campaigns.
