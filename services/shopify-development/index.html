09 / FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONSSHOPIFY DEVELOPMENT USA

Questions before we get started.

Do you provide Shopify development for businesses in the USA?

Yes. I work remotely with ecommerce businesses across the United States, including brands based in New York, Los Angeles, Chicago, Austin, Miami and other locations.

Can you customize my existing Shopify theme?

Yes. I can review your current theme and customize sections, templates, product pages, collection layouts, navigation, cart interactions and responsive styling based on the scope.

Do you develop custom Shopify themes using Liquid?

Yes. Shopify Liquid, JSON templates, sections, blocks, HTML, CSS and JavaScript can be used to create tailored storefront experiences and reusable theme components.

Can you build a Shopify store from scratch?

Yes. A new store project can include theme selection or custom theme work, page layouts, navigation, product and collection templates, content sections and launch-readiness checks. Product data, copy and account access are coordinated with you.

Can you improve my product pages and collection pages?

Yes. Product page work can include media layouts, variant selection, metafield content, accordions, trust details and related products. Collection work can include grids, sorting, filters and product discovery improvements.

Can you connect apps, payments and shipping tools?

Compatible apps and supported services can be integrated or configured. Payment provider eligibility, shipping coverage, tax obligations and account approvals depend on your business, state and chosen providers, so these are confirmed with your team.

Will my Shopify store be SEO-friendly?

I can implement on-page SEO foundations including metadata, heading structure, descriptive image alt text, internal links, clean content hierarchy and technical checks. Shopify also provides built-in SEO features. Rankings are not guaranteed and depend on competition, authority and ongoing content work.

Will the store work well on mobile?

Mobile-first implementation and testing are part of the process. Key pages, menus, product options, cart interactions, buttons and forms are checked at common mobile widths.

How do I get a Shopify development quote?

Send your store URL (if available), product category, required features, reference websites and target launch timeline. I can then help define the scope and next steps.
