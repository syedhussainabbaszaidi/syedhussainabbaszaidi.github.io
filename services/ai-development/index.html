08 / FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONSAI DEVELOPMENT FAQ

Good questions
before you build.

What does custom AI development include?

Custom AI development can include discovery, solution architecture, AI model or API integration, chatbot and agent development, data connections, workflow automation, application interfaces, testing and deployment. The exact scope depends on your requirements.

Do you provide AI development services to businesses in the USA?

Yes. US businesses can work with me remotely on AI software development, AI integrations, chatbots, AI agents and automation projects. Project scope, communication and delivery milestones are agreed before work begins.

How much does custom AI software development cost?

AI development cost depends on the use case, integrations, data readiness, model usage, security needs and delivery scope. A focused prototype is different from a production application connected to multiple systems. Share your requirements to discuss an appropriate estimate.

How long does an AI development project take?

Timelines depend on complexity, access to data and systems, review cycles and the features required. A small proof of concept may take less time than a production-ready platform with authentication, monitoring and multiple integrations. A timeline can be estimated after discovery.

Can you integrate AI into an existing website or software?

Yes. AI capabilities can be integrated into existing websites, ecommerce experiences, SaaS products and internal applications through APIs and suitable backend workflows, subject to the platform and technical constraints.

Can you build an AI chatbot using our company documents?

Yes. A knowledge-based chatbot can be designed to retrieve answers from approved company content and documents. The project should define source permissions, content freshness, answer evaluation and when the chatbot should hand a question to a person.

Which AI models and technologies do you use?

The right technology depends on the project. Options may include OpenAI and other LLM APIs, retrieval-augmented generation, Python or PHP backends, JavaScript interfaces, databases and third-party integrations. Model selection is based on fit, cost, quality and requirements.

How do you approach AI security and data privacy?

Security and privacy requirements should be reviewed during discovery. The implementation can consider data minimization, access control, secure API handling, retention policies and human review. Specific regulatory obligations must be assessed for each business and use case.

Will an AI system always give accurate answers?

No AI system should be assumed to be error-free. Good implementations use relevant source data, evaluation, guardrails, clear user expectations and human escalation for uncertain or high-impact cases.

What do you need to provide an AI project estimate?

Share the problem you want to solve, who will use the system, your current tools or website, key features, example workflows, integrations, preferred timeline and any data or security constraints.
