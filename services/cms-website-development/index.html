07 / CMS WEBSITE FAQsCOMMON QUESTIONS

Questions,
answered.

Which CMS is best for a US business website?

It depends on the project. WordPress is flexible for content-led and business websites, Shopify is purpose-built for managed ecommerce, and Webflow or other platforms may fit specific visual publishing workflows.

Can you redesign my existing CMS website?

Yes. The work can focus on design, navigation, mobile experience, content structure, performance or selected functionality, depending on the current platform and access.

Can you migrate my website to WordPress or Shopify?

Migration can be planned after reviewing the source platform, page and product volume, URL structure, data export options and integrations. Redirects and content checks should be part of the plan.

Will my team be able to edit website content?

Yes. The editing experience can be planned with reusable sections, clear content fields and suitable user roles based on the selected CMS.

Do you provide CMS website development for US companies remotely?

Yes. Projects can be discussed and delivered remotely for businesses across the United States, with scope, communication and milestones agreed before work begins.
