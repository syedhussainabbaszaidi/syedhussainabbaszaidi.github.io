07 / FAQSLET’S GET YOUR PROJECT MOVING

Questions
before we start?

How can I contact you about a project?

Use the WhatsApp buttons across the website or visit the contact section. You can also connect through LinkedIn or Fiverr. WhatsApp is the quickest way to share your requirements.

What should I include in my first message?

Tell me what you want to build or improve, your business or industry, the features you need, your preferred timeline and any reference websites or examples.

Can I request a website, AI solution or custom software quote?

Yes. Share your brief and I’ll discuss the scope, suitable approach, expected deliverables and a project estimate before work begins.

Do you work with clients outside Pakistan?

Yes. Project discussions can be handled remotely, and I’m open to working with clients internationally.

Can you improve my existing website instead of building a new one?

Yes. Existing Shopify, WordPress, CMS and custom websites can be reviewed for improvements, new features, performance and user experience.

How does the project process work?

We start with your requirements, agree on scope and deliverables, plan the work, build the solution and review it with you before launch or handover.

How do I get started today?

Click “Discuss your project” or “Chat on WhatsApp”, send your brief and reference links, and we can take it from there.
