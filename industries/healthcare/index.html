05 / FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONSHEALTHCARE PROJECTS

Questions,
answered.

Can you build a website for a US medical practice?

Yes. The site can include provider profiles, specialty pages, locations, patient resources and appointment request paths.

Can you create a HIPAA-compliant patient portal?

I can develop portal interfaces and integrations, but HIPAA compliance depends on the full architecture, vendors, policies, contracts and operational controls. It must be reviewed by qualified compliance and security professionals.

Can healthcare websites include appointment forms?

Yes. The form design and data flow should be reviewed carefully to avoid collecting sensitive information through unsuitable channels.

Can you help with healthcare SEO?

Yes. Work can include specialty landing pages, local information, technical SEO and helpful FAQs based on actual services.

Do you work with healthcare technology vendors?

Yes. Product websites, B2B landing pages, portals and integration-focused experiences can be scoped for healthtech companies.
